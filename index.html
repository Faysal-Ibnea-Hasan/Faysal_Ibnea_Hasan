Most of what I ship is invisible — and that is the point.

A customer sees a storefront load. What is actually happening is a tenancy middleware deciding which vendor's world this request belongs to, a pricing snapshot being frozen so a booking can never silently change cost, and a queue worker somewhere preparing the invoice.

That is the work I have spent the last two years on: commercially licensed SaaS products sold to businesses worldwide, where a mistake in a commission calculation is somebody's actual money.

I care most about the boundary problems. Who is allowed to see this row. What happens when two servicemen are assigned the same hour. What the number should be when a refund, a cashback and a commission all touch the same order.
